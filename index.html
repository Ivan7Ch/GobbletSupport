Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play online with a friend?

Open Gorshok, tap Play with People, then Invite a Friend. One player chooses Create a game and shares the 6-digit code (tap the code to copy it). The other player chooses Join a game and enters that code. Both players need an active internet connection for the match to sync.

What are lightnings for?

Creating an online game with a friend costs 1 lightning, charged only once your friend has joined. Joining a friend's game is free. If you run out, you can watch a short video to get more.

My opponent's move isn't showing up / the board seems frozen.

This is usually a connection hiccup. Check that both players have a stable connection. If the board doesn't recover, return to the main menu and start a new game.

How does the AI opponent work?

You can play against the built-in AI any time from the main menu, no connection required. Pick a difficulty that suits you. It plays a full strategic game, including covering your pieces with larger ones.

What do Strategy and Rating mean at the end of a game?

Strategy shows how accurate your moves were during the game. Rating is based on that accuracy, so the better your moves, the higher your rating.

I found a bug or the app crashed.

Please email us with a short description of what happened and, if possible, what you were doing right before it occurred. That detail helps us track it down quickly.

How do I delete my data or account?

Email us from the address you'd like removed, and we'll delete your match history and any associated data.
